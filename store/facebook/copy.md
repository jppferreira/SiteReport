## About / longer intro (About tab)

Trade Report turns before-and-after photos, work notes, and an optional customer signature into a branded PDF — before you leave the job.

Built for trades and small service businesses. No account. No cloud. Jobs, photos, and reports stay on the device until you choose to share or back up.

Free for 3 jobs. Pro unlocks unlimited reports, your logo and colours, and customer sign-off.

Website: https://tradereportapp.com/
