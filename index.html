Privacy

The job never leaves the phone.

Trade Report has no sign-in and no cloud database. Customer names, addresses, notes, photos, signatures, and reports stay in the app’s storage on that device unless you share a PDF or save a backup yourself. A backup is a zip you confirm first, then save to a folder you pick, such as your own Google Drive. The app does not sign in to Google. Privacy policy.
